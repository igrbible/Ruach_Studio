Artwork

Who paints a take's artwork: the player's, the cards', the MP3's cover. A drawing waits in the queue for a card with room.

Qwen-Image 2512 (Apache-2.0) paints a cover with Lightning (lightx2v, Apache-2.0) when its file is here, and every picture passes a content filter; Qwen-Image 2.1 is under Qwen's research licence: research and evaluation only, not for a song that is sold. Times on an RTX 3090, the prompt and the look-over not counted.

MIDI

A MIDI keyboard plays the piano roll; a controller's control port (an M-Audio Oxygen's DAW port in its Mackie preset, or any Mackie Control) runs the transport: the roll's, the DAW's in sight, else the player's. Early support: made to the Mackie Control protocol and tried through the browser's MIDI, not yet on every device.

A plugin may bring a script for its own controller: plugin.json, "midi" (PLUGINS.md, «MIDI scripts»).

          Refiner
          the take in hand, heard and mended: spectrum, artifacts, stems, remaster, upscale, lyrics check
      pick a take on the right
      
      
          
    
    
          
      Import a TrackWAV, FLAC, MP3… from anywhere: it becomes a take in the library (48 kHz 24-bit), marked imported, ready for stems, remaster and the rest.
      
          
        
          
          
          
        
          
        
        
          
          
          
        
          
      
          
    
          

    
    
          
      
      
          
        StemsSplit on Forge: BS-Roformer for the voice, htdemucs_ft for drums, bass and the rest. Each file plays, goes into the spectrum and the lyrics check.
        
          
          
          
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
          
          
        
          
      
          

      
      
          
        DAWbetaA set of stems on its lanes: level, mute and solo, a version for each stem, silence drawn on a wave, compression, the room and the delay; heard here as you set it, then mixed on Forge into a branch of the tree.
        
          
      
          

      
      
          
        Digital GatewayThe take, or any file made from it, through an analog character in numbers on Forge: tape, console, tube, transformer or vinyl. Its curve oversampled, its tone, its noise, its movement in time; as loud as it came. The analog gateway's twin, without the gear.
        
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          
          
          
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
          
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          About a tenth of the song's length on Forge's CPU at 4×. The tree says what it adds to a −12 dBFS tone (the 2nd and 3rd harmonics) and its loudness in and out; a branch of it can be the analog gateway's source, the Remaster's, or another gateway's.
          
        
          
      
          

      
      
          
        RemasterThe Debunker v6 on Forge: mix the stems or take the take, clean, de-ess, 432 Hz, loudness. Each run is a new branch of the tree.
        
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          
          
          
            
            
            
            
          
          
          
          
            
            
            
          
          
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          
            
            
            
            
          
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          Loudness in two passes (measured, then a linear gain), 48 kHz out. The result appears in the Stems tree above, with its loudness in and out.
          
        
          
      
          

      
      
          
        UpscaleUniverSR on Forge draws the top of the spectrum anew. Each run is a branch of the tree; its variants differ by the noise.
        
          
          
          
            
            
            
          
          
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          About 1.6 times the length of the audio per variant on a 3090, in 20 s pieces joined by 1 s crossfades (measured: no step at a join beyond the difference between two renders).
          
        
          
      
          

      
      
          
        DebuzzYuE2's decoder writes sound in frames of 1920 samples, 25 a second, and its highs tremble with them: the metallic buzz. This takes out the part locked to that frame clock, above 2 kHz, the lows untouched. Before Upscale. On a track from elsewhere it finds nothing, and says so.
        
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          About a minute for seven, on the CPU. The tree shows the ripple measured in and out: the 4–12 kHz envelope folded on 1920 samples, against 1900 and 1940 as controls (what any sound shows by chance).
          
        
          
      
          

      
      
          
        ArtifactsWhat may be wrong in the sound: a tone that will not leave, the VAE's 25-frame buzz in the highs, clicks, clipping, dropouts, stereo that fights itself. A time plays from there.
        
          
          
          
            
            
            
          
          
          
          
        
          
      
          

      
      
          
        SpectrumWhere the sound sits: spectrogram, average spectrum, band energy. Lay another take over it to compare.
        
          
          
          
            
          
              
              
              
              
            
          
            
            
          
          
          
          
          
            
            
          
            
          
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
        
          
      
          

      
      
          
        Lyrics CheckWhisper listens to this take on Forge and matches what it hears to your lyrics, minute by minute.
        
          
          
          
            
            
            
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
            
          
              Karaoke timing
              
              
              
              ⤓ .lrc
              ⤓ .lrc (words)
              ⤓ .srt
            
          
            
            
          
            
          
            Per minute: the mean match to the closest lyric line, 0.6 and up clear, below 0.4 glossolalia. "Reached" is the furthest line matched, not a count of lines sung. A click on a minute or a time plays from there.
