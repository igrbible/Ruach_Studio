# YuE2 · the model's mark, for the credits

The About card on the Engine page credits YuE2 under its own mark (HERESY 1166; Viktor 04.10.2026: «В колонке YuE2 —
логотип так же, как в первой наш»). The mark is M-A-P's: the circle with the notes and **YuE²**, as on the project page
(map-yue2.github.io, `yue-mark.svg`). Viktor downloaded it as a picture (`Yue2_Logo.webp`, 1454×817, a beige ground and
much air around it); these files are that picture traced: each ink split from the ground at its half-way line, three
times enlarged, traced by Inkscape's tracer (potrace), cropped to the ink, the points kept to a tenth of a source pixel.

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `yue2-logo.svg` | for a light ground: the colours of the original (#9E4E37 the circle and the ², #362A20 the word), no ground |
| `yue2-logo-on-dark.svg` | for a dark ground: the word light (#F2ECE2), the terracotta lifted (#D0724F) |
| `yue2-logo.png`, `yue2-logo-on-dark.png` | the same as pictures, 1200×399, transparent |

In the page the mark is inline (index.html, the About card): the word takes the theme's ink, the circle and the ² a
terracotta drawn a little toward the ink (app.css, `.yue2-mark`). The banner Viktor downloaded beside it (the orange disc,
«From lyrics to full songs») is yue2ai.app's, a paid service built on the model, not the model's makers: it is not used.
