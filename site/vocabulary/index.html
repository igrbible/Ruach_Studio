The First Wave's vocabulary

Click words to build a style: they line up in the order the model learned.

Nobody wrote these: LAION's Empathic Insight listened to three 30-second windows of every track and scored 55 dimensions. Each was measured against the whole set, so a word means «more than most of this set»: a track took the words of its dimensions above one standard deviation, the strongest first, at most four. The voice's qualities went only to tracks with a voice.
